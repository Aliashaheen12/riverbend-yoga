About Riverbend

Riverbend opened in 2015 with one goal: to give our neighbors a peaceful place to slow down and care for themselves, no experience required.

Our Instructors

Maya Chen, Lead Instructor

Maya has taught for twelve years and specializes in gentle and chair yoga for older adults. She believes the best pose is the one that feels good for you today.

Daniel Osei, Vinyasa & Lunchtime Express

Daniel helps busy professionals release desk tension with efficient, energizing flows. His evening classes are known for their calming final relaxation.

Rosa Alvarez, Restorative & Yin

Rosa leads slow, deeply restful classes and our monthly workshops. She is trained in trauma-informed teaching and breath-focused practice.

Accessibility Accommodations

  • Step-free entrance and an accessible restroom
  • Chairs, blocks, straps, and bolsters available in every class
  • Chair-based options offered for every standing sequence
  • Instructors give clear verbal cues for students with low vision

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first class.
  • Cancel at least 4 hours before class to keep your credit.
  • Mats are available to borrow at no charge.
  • Let your instructor know about any injuries or health concerns before class begins.
